Exocrine and Endocrine Systems

Overview

The exocrine and endocrine systems are both made up of glands, but they work in different ways.

Exocrine glands release substances through ducts, while endocrine glands release hormones directly into the bloodstream.

Exocrine System

The exocrine system produces substances that are used in specific parts of the body such as the skin, mouth and digestive system.

  • Mammary glands: Produce milk
  • Salivary glands: Produce saliva for digestion
  • Sweat glands: Help control body temperature
  • Sebaceous glands: Produce oil for skin and hair
  • Mucosal glands: Protect internal tissues

Endocrine System

The endocrine system produces hormones, which act as messengers that control body processes like growth, metabolism and balance.

  • Pituitary gland: Controls other glands
  • Pineal gland: Produces melatonin
  • Thyroid gland: Controls metabolism
  • Adrenal glands: Help manage stress and blood pressure

Importance

These systems help regulate and maintain balance in the body, ensuring everything works together properly.
