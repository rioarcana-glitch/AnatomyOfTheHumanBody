The Digestive System

Overview

The digestive system is a group of organs that work together to break down food and absorb nutrients needed for survival, growth and repair. It includes the gastrointestinal tract and accessory organs like the liver and pancreas.

Main Parts

  • Mouth
  • Oesophagus
  • Stomach
  • Small intestine
  • Large intestine
  • Liver & Pancreas

The Mouth

Food enters the body here. Teeth break it down mechanically while saliva begins chemical digestion. The tongue helps move food for swallowing.

Oesophagus & Stomach

The oesophagus moves food using peristalsis. In the stomach, strong acid (pH ~1) and enzymes break food down further while mucus protects the lining.

Small Intestine

Most nutrients are absorbed here. Enzymes and bile break food into tiny particles which are absorbed into the bloodstream by villi.

Large Intestine

Absorbs water and electrolytes and forms waste. It also produces vitamins like B and K.

Key Organs

The liver produces bile and processes nutrients, while the pancreas releases digestive enzymes that help break down food.
