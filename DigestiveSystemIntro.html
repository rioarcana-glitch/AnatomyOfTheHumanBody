The Digestive System

Overview

The digestive system is a group of organs that work together to break down food and absorb nutrients needed for energy, growth and repair. It includes the gastrointestinal tract and organs like the liver and pancreas. Digestion happens through mechanical processes (like chewing) and chemical processes using enzymes. Different parts of the system have different pH levels so enzymes can work properly.

Main Parts

  • Mouth
  • Oesophagus
  • Stomach
  • Small intestine
  • Large intestine
  • Liver
  • Pancreas
  • Gallbladder
  • Spleen (not directly part of the system)
  • Rectum and anus

The Mouth

Digestion starts in the mouth. Teeth break food into smaller pieces while saliva begins to break down carbohydrates. The tongue helps move the food and makes it easier to swallow.

The Oesophagus and Stomach

The oesophagus moves food to the stomach using wave-like movements called peristalsis. In the stomach, food is mixed with strong acid and enzymes. The acid helps break down food, and the stomach lining protects itself with mucus.

The Liver

The liver has many important roles. It produces bile to help digest fats, controls blood sugar by storing and releasing glucose, and removes toxins from the body. It also produces important proteins and helps manage nutrients.

The Small Intestine

Most nutrients are absorbed in the small intestine. Enzymes from the pancreas and bile from the liver break food down into very small particles. Tiny structures called villi absorb these nutrients into the bloodstream.

The Large Intestine

The large intestine absorbs water and electrolytes and helps produce vitamins like B and K. It turns leftover material into waste. The appendix helps store helpful bacteria for digestion.

The Gallbladder and Spleen

The gallbladder stores bile made by the liver and releases it to help break down fats. The spleen is not part of the digestive system but helps filter blood and fight infections.

The Rectum and Anus

The rectum stores waste until it is ready to leave the body. The anus is where waste is released. The whole digestive process usually takes between 14 and 58 hours.
