Process

  1. Blood is filtered in the kidneys
  2. Waste and water travel through the ureters to the bladder
  3. Urine is stored in the bladder until it is released through the urethra

Key Facts

The kidneys filter over 100 litres of blood every day and produce around 1–2 litres of urine.

Pee Colours

The colour of urine depends mainly on hydration levels. Less water leads to darker urine.

A pigment called urochrome also affects colour, and foods or health conditions can change it too.
